Pitfalls to Watch

Trap 1 · Full (0.0 surplus) ≠ Hot: When demand = 2.0 and Cap = 2.0, surplus = 0. A tile is oversubscribed only when surplus > 0 strictly.
Trap 2 · Forgetting Which Cap You Used: Lowering Cap from 2.0 to 1.0 shrinks supply for the same wires, flipping borderline tiles to hot. Always record units and Cap.
Trap 3 · Trusting an Isolated 0.0 Tile: A tile with 0.0 demand can still sit boxed inside a hot neighborhood—always inspect its neighboring GCells.
Trap 4 · Heat Map Without a Tile List: Always list oversubscribed tiles (col, row) with exact demand − capacity numbers before trusting color shading alone.
